# Example Prompt: Diabetic Sarcopenia

## User prompt

```text
基于 GEO 数据库，对糖尿病肌少症进行转录组全栈式分析。请设计完整课题，包括数据集检索、差异表达、富集分析、WGCNA、PPI、机器学习筛选诊断基因、免疫浸润、药物预测和外部验证方案。
```

## Expected assistant behavior

The assistant should generate:

1. A research title
2. Scientific hypothesis
3. GEO search strategy
4. Inclusion and exclusion criteria
5. Microarray and RNA-seq preprocessing plan
6. Differential-expression analysis strategy
7. GO/KEGG/GSEA plan
8. WGCNA plan
9. PPI and hub-gene discovery plan
10. Machine-learning biomarker screening strategy
11. ROC and external validation plan
12. Immune-infiltration plan
13. Mitochondrial, metabolic and cell-death analysis modules
14. Regulatory-network plan
15. Drug-prediction and molecular-docking plan
16. Manuscript-ready methods framework

## Example project title

Integrated Bioinformatics and Machine-Learning Analysis Identifies Immune- and Mitochondria-Related Biomarkers in Diabetic Sarcopenia.

## Example hypothesis

Diabetic sarcopenia may be driven by the interaction of insulin-resistance signaling, mitochondrial dysfunction, chronic inflammation and muscle-protein degradation. Integrated GEO transcriptome analysis can identify diagnostic genes and mechanistic pathways for further experimental validation.
